2. Local storage of your tasks

All tasks, intervals, notes and settings you create are stored only locally on your device. There is no user account, and this data is not transmitted to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Data backup (export)

Using the “Export data” function you can share your tasks as a file (e.g. via email or cloud). This action is triggered only by you, and you choose the destination.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may process personal data for this, including the Advertising ID, device information and IP address, to serve and measure ads.

  • Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can limit personalized ads or reset your Advertising ID in your device settings under “Ads”.

The optional “Premium” purchase removes ads permanently.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time Premium purchase is handled by Google Play. Payment and your payment details are processed solely by Google; we receive no payment or card data. Google’s privacy terms apply.

6. Notifications

The app can remind you locally about due tasks. These notifications are generated on your device; no data is sent to us. You can disable reminders at any time in the app or in your system settings.

7. Permissions

  • Internet – only to load ads.
  • Notifications – to remind you about due tasks (Android 13+).

8. No sharing or selling

We do not sell data and do not share data for advertising with third parties, apart from the processing by Google described above (AdMob, Play).

9. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since we do not store personal data about you ourselves, these rights mainly concern the data processed by Google – please also contact Google for those. For app-related matters, reach us at the email above.
